Nighthawk is a fusion crawler that walks the web itself, reads raw crawl archives and queries every structured engine it can reach — then extracts, indexes and ranks the hosted files, open calls and portals that a normal results page never shows you.

!
This engine does not rank by popularity, and it does not filter like one.

The point of Nighthawk is the material a normal results page demotes or drops: the PDF in /uploads/2019/03/, the tender notice behind a portal search, the “Index of /” listing nobody linked, the spreadsheet in a data dump, the page the site deleted. No freshness gate, no “helpful content” scorer, no domain-authority penalty. Queries are matched word for word — no stemming, no synonyms, no expansion — and nothing is removed from the result set: duplicates are reported, junk is labelled and sorted last. Sort order can change; that policy does not.

    Deep links — the results only a pasted URL can reach

    no search engine involved

    A portal’s search form, an unlinked annex, a sitemap entry nothing points at, an attachment behind a JavaScript link, a page the site deleted — none of that can be ranked by an engine that never crawled it. Paste the exact links and Nighthawk crawls from them and then tells you, per result, why nothing else could have found it. Same rules as the rest of the engine: matches are verbatim, ordering is newest-first, and nothing is removed.
